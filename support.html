Reporting a problem

You can report bugs directly inside the app: open Settings → Submit a Bug. Including a short description of what happened and which game you were playing helps us fix it faster.

Common questions

How do I play with friends?
Choose Online, create a room, and share the room code. Your friends enter the same code to join.

What are chips?
Chips are an in-app IOU scoring system for keeping track of results between friends. They involve no real money and have no cash value.

I lost my account / progress.
DoopleDeck accounts are tied to your device. Reinstalling the app or switching devices creates a new account, and previous progress cannot be recovered. If you need help, email us using the address above.
